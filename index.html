About

I’m a researcher in medical robotics and AI-driven surgical guidance, currently a Malone Postdoctoral Fellow at Johns Hopkins University with Dr. Russell H. Taylor and Dr. Axel Krieger. My work focuses on integrating imaging, robotics, and intelligent systems for precision cancer surgery. I completed my Ph.D. in July 2025 at Queen's University in the Perk and Med-i Lab with Dr. Gabor Fichtinger and Dr. Parvin Mousavi, externally mentored by Dr. Russell H. Taylor.
